# FUTURE_DS_01

## Business Sales Performance Analytics

### Objective
Analyze business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-performing products, and regional performance.

### Tools Used
- Tableau
- Online Retail Dataset (CSV)

### Key Findings
- Total Revenue: 9,747,748
- Total Orders: 22,061
- Revenue peaked in November 2011.
- DOTCOM POSTAGE was the top revenue-generating product.
- United Kingdom generated the highest revenue.

### Recommendations
- Increase inventory before peak demand periods.
- Focus marketing on top-performing products.
- Expand sales efforts beyond the UK.
- Improve customer retention through targeted campaigns.
